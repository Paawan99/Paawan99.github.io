01 — About

I make numbers
tell stories

I'm Paawan Shah. I did my BBA from NMIMS, Mumbai and went on to complete my MBA at University Canada West (UCW) in Vancouver. That combination gave me something I value a lot, a global perspective grounded in real fundamentals.

I've spent the last few years working in finance and retail, and honestly, that's where I learned the most. Not from textbooks, but from actually managing cash flows, building budgets from scratch, figuring out why certain product lines underperform, and presenting those findings to people who needed to make fast decisions. I've sat in rooms where the numbers had to tell a story, and I made sure they did.

What drives me is the intersection of financial analysis and clear communication. I can build a DCF model, run a variance analysis, or break down a set of financial statements, but what I really care about is making that work mean something to the person reading it. Numbers without context are just noise.

Right now, I'm focused on deepening my skills in equity research, valuation, and portfolio strategy, and building the kind of portfolio that speaks for itself. This site is part of that.
