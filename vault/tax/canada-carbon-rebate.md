# Canada Carbon Rebate

tags: tax, benefits, newcomer

## What it is

The Canada Carbon Rebate (formerly known as the Climate Action Incentive Payment) is a tax-free payment intended to return proceeds from the federal fuel charge to individuals and families in provinces where it applies. It's designed to offset the cost impact of the federal carbon pricing system on households, and most households received back as much or more than they pay, according to CRA/government figures.

## Quarterly payments

Like the GST/HST credit, the Canada Carbon Rebate is generally paid quarterly. Because federal carbon pricing rules and provincial participation have changed over time, availability of this rebate can change year to year — verify current status and payment dates on canada.ca before relying on it.

## It varies by province

The rebate only applies in provinces where the federal fuel charge applies (this has historically included provinces without their own equivalent carbon pricing system). The dollar amount also varies by province, since it's tied to the fuel charge collected in that province. Residents of provinces with their own carbon pricing systems (rather than the federal backstop) may not receive this federal payment at all.

## Base amount plus rural supplement

The rebate typically includes a base amount for an individual, with additional set amounts for a spouse/common-law partner and for each child. Residents of small and rural communities often qualify for an additional rural supplement, meant to reflect higher energy needs and reduced access to alternative transportation. Exact base amounts and the rural supplement percentage are set annually and by province, and these figures change yearly — verify on canada.ca.

## How eligibility works

There's typically no separate application: eligibility is determined automatically from your filed tax return, similar to the GST/HST credit. You generally need to file a tax return (even with no income) and, if applicable, indicate on your return that you live in a small or rural community to be considered for the supplement. Newcomers should file a return (or the equivalent newcomer benefit application) as soon as they're eligible to be considered.
