# Transfer Speed, Limits, and Verification

tags: remittance, kyc, verification, transfer limits, speed

## Typical speeds

Digital-first providers (like fintech apps) commonly complete transfers within minutes to one business day for bank-to-bank transfers in well-established corridors, and can be near-instant for cash pickup or mobile wallet delivery in supported countries. Traditional bank wires typically take one to five business days, partly because they route through intermediary correspondent banks. Speed can vary by the receiving country's banking hours, holidays, and the specific delivery method chosen.

## Transfer limits

Most providers set per-transaction and cumulative (daily, monthly, or annual) sending limits, which are often lower for new or unverified accounts and increase once you complete identity verification. Very large transfers may require additional documentation (source of funds) regardless of provider, and cash-pickup methods often have lower limits than bank-deposit methods for security reasons.

## Verification and KYC

Regulated money transfer providers must perform Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification — typically government-issued photo ID, and for larger amounts, proof of address or source of funds — as part of anti-money-laundering compliance. In Canada, this is required under FINTRAC regulations; expect to verify your identity before your first transfer or before exceeding certain thresholds, and keep your documents current, since incomplete verification is a common reason transfers are delayed or held.
