# Comparing Money Transfer Providers

tags: remittance, wise, remitly, bank wire, comparison

## Different business models

Bank wires typically use their own established SWIFT network and legacy exchange-rate desks, often resulting in higher markups but perceived (sometimes overstated) security from an established institution. Fintech providers such as Wise operate more like a matching network with transparent, published exchange-rate margins and flat or percentage fees shown upfront. Providers such as Remitly (and similar cash-pickup/digital-wallet-focused services) often compete on speed and destination convenience (cash pickup, mobile wallet deposit) and may offer promotional rates for first transfers, with margins that vary more by corridor and delivery method.

## What actually matters: amount received

The only number that matters when comparing providers is the amount your recipient actually receives in their local currency, for your specific corridor (sending country/currency to receiving country/currency), amount, and delivery method (bank deposit, cash pickup, mobile wallet). Two providers with identical advertised fees can produce very different amounts received if their exchange-rate markups differ, so always run the same transfer amount through each provider's calculator before choosing.

## Practical comparison checklist

When comparing, check: (1) the exact amount received after all fees and the exchange rate, (2) whether the fee or rate is promotional/first-time-only versus the ongoing rate, (3) delivery speed and method, (4) any caps on transfer amount without extra verification, and (5) whether the provider is registered with Canada's financial crimes regulator (FINTRAC) or the equivalent regulator in your country. Because promotional rates and fee structures change frequently, re-check current rates each time you send a larger transfer rather than assuming last month's comparison still holds.
