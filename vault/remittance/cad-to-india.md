# Sending Money from Canada to India (CAD to INR)

tags: remittance, cad, inr, india, corridor

## A high-volume corridor for newcomers

The Canada-to-India corridor is one of the largest remittance corridors used by Canadian newcomers, students, and workers supporting family back home, which means it's also one of the most competitive — many providers offer specialized rates, faster delivery, or promotions specifically for CAD-to-INR transfers because of the volume involved.

## What to check for this corridor

Because competition is high, it's worth comparing several providers specifically for CAD-to-INR rather than assuming a general "best overall" provider is best for this corridor: check the exact INR amount the recipient will receive for your CAD amount, whether delivery is by bank deposit (NEFT/IMPS/RTGS-style crediting to an Indian bank account), UPI-based transfer, or cash pickup, and how quickly the recipient's bank actually credits the funds (which can differ from the sender-side "transfer complete" time).

## Practical tips

Delivery to Indian bank accounts generally requires the recipient's IFSC code and account number; double-check both since incorrect details can delay or misdirect funds. Some providers offer better rates for larger transfer amounts (tiered pricing), so consolidating multiple smaller sends into fewer larger ones can reduce the effective percentage cost. As with any corridor, rates and promotions change frequently, so re-compare before each large transfer rather than relying on a rate you saw previously.
