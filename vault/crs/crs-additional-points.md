# CRS Additional Points

tags: crs, express entry, pnp, french, canadian study, arranged employment

## Provincial nomination: +600

A nomination from a Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) aligned with Express Entry adds 600 CRS points — enough to effectively guarantee an Invitation to Apply (ITA) in the next relevant draw, since 600 points puts a candidate above nearly every other score in the pool. See the dedicated PNP note for how nominations work.

## French language bonus: +25 or +50

Candidates with strong French results (commonly CLB 7 or higher on all four abilities) can receive bonus points, with a lower bonus for French-only strength and a higher bonus (roughly 50 points) if you also have strong English results (commonly CLB 5 or higher). This bonus was introduced to support French-speaking immigration outside Quebec and has become a significant lever, especially combined with French-language category draws.

## Sibling in Canada: +15

If you or your spouse/common-law partner has a brother or sister who is a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, living in Canada, and at least 18 years old, you may claim 15 additional points. Only one sibling relationship can be claimed, and specific relationship and residency conditions apply.

## Canadian study experience: +15 or +30

Completing a credential from a Canadian post-secondary institution can add points: shorter programs (roughly one or two years) typically earn 15 points, while longer programs (three years or more, including most bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees) typically earn 30 points. This is separate from, and in addition to, education points and skill-transferability combinations.

## Important: arranged employment points were removed in spring 2025

Historically, a valid, qualifying job offer (arranged employment) could add significant CRS points. IRCC removed arranged employment points from the CRS as of the changes rolled out in spring 2025 — a job offer may still support eligibility for certain programs, but it no longer directly adds CRS points. Always verify the current rules on the official IRCC page before relying on any job-offer point value.
