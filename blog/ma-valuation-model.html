M&A Valuation Model

Interactive Merger & Acquisition Analysis Tool

This interactive tool models mergers and acquisitions by combining real company financial data with professional valuation methodologies. The three main approaches to M&A valuation help investors understand the fair value of a deal.

The comparable company analysis uses market multiples from similar companies to establish valuation ranges. Discounted cash flow analysis projects future cash flows and determines intrinsic value based on growth assumptions. Finally, the accretion and dilution analysis evaluates whether a deal immediately improves or reduces the acquirer's earnings per share.

We apply market multiples from each metric to estimate a range of fair values for the target company.

💰 DCF Valuation

The Discounted Cash Flow method projects the target company's future free cash flows over five years and calculates a terminal value based on perpetual growth. This intrinsic value reflects what the business is worth based on its ability to generate cash. The discount rate represents the required return based on risk, commonly called the Weighted Average Cost of Capital or WACC.

📈 Accretion / Dilution Analysis

When the acquirer buys the target company, the immediate impact on the acquirer's earnings per share depends on the deal structure and premium paid. An accretive deal increases EPS, while a dilutive deal decreases it. This analysis helps investors understand the short term financial impact of the transaction independent of any long term synergies.
