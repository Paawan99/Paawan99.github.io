# CRS Overview (Comprehensive Ranking System)

tags: crs, express entry, immigration, ircc

## What CRS is

The Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) is the points-based test that Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) uses to rank candidates in the Express Entry pool. Everyone eligible for Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker, Canadian Experience Class, or Federal Skilled Trades) gets a CRS score, and IRCC periodically invites the highest-ranked candidates to apply for permanent residence through an "Invitation to Apply" (ITA) draw.

## Maximum 1,200 points

The CRS score has a maximum of 1,200 points, split into two broad parts: up to 600 points from "core/human capital" factors (plus spouse factors, if applicable) and up to 600 points from "additional" factors such as a provincial nomination, a valid job offer (largely phased out — see below), or Canadian education. In practice, most candidates without a provincial nomination score well below 600 total.

## The four factor buckets

CRS points come from four buckets: (1) core human capital factors — age, education, official language proficiency, and Canadian work experience; (2) spouse or common-law partner factors — their education, language, and Canadian work experience, which only apply if you have an accompanying spouse; (3) skill transferability factors — combinations of education, language, and work experience that can add up to 100 extra points; and (4) additional points — for a provincial nomination, French language ability, a sibling in Canada, or Canadian study experience.

## It's an estimate, not a guarantee

Any CRS calculator, including a copilot like this one, produces an estimate for educational purposes only. Your actual, official score is calculated by IRCC when you submit a complete Express Entry profile, and only IRCC's tool and rules are authoritative. Rules, cut-off scores, and point values change over time (for example, arranged employment points were removed in 2025), so always verify current figures and eligibility on the official IRCC website before making decisions.
